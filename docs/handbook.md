# 托福口语与写作备考手册

整理日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日  
计划修订日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日
考试计划：2026 年十二月底  
目标：原 0–30 分制口语和写作各 25 分；按 ETS 对照表，两项均落在新制 5 分档。分档对照并非单题分数换算。

本手册先整理当前目录中的 30 篇学术讨论写作素材与 6 张截图，再按现行题型补充场景表达和自编迁移练习。重点是 Email 和 Discussion。短语需要和适用语境、完整句子一起掌握，不能只记中文释义。

## 使用说明

1. 先看中文情境，主动说出或写出英文，再展开答案。
2. 用自己的内容替换例句中的人物、时间、对象与理由。
3. 写作要说明具体问题、原因和后果；口语要用个人例子展开。
4. 听后复述必须准确复现实际听到的句子，不主动改写。本手册例句是练习材料，不是预测题。
5. Interview 练习默认 45 秒，可调整；这里只用于训练，不代表完整考试模拟。
6. 每次练习按“不记得／有点印象／记得”安排复习。认得不等于能在新题中使用。
7. 新例句和参考回答均为自编，不是 ETS 官方答案或声称的真实个人经历。个人经历类回答应换成自己的内容。

### 纠错和使用风险

| 原表达或问题 | 修正与处理 |
|---|---|
| Once receive university offers | Once students receive university offers, ... |
| coordination of body | improve coordination |
| make and maintain relationship with others | build and maintain relationships |
| drive away my lassitude | help me feel less tired |
| help students to avoiding taking detours | help students avoid unnecessary mistakes |
| constitute to a proportion | account for a small proportion of ... |
| simulate their rude behaviors | imitate their rude behavior |
| mitigate the traffic jam | ease traffic congestion |
| beat around the bushes | beat around the bush，固定习语 |
| Listening to TED allow me ... | Listening to TED talks allows me ... |
| Designing questionnaires is familiar task ... | Designing questionnaires is a familiar task ... |
| people with malicious intent is an exception | people with malicious intent are an exception |
| be familiar to 与 be familiar with | The task is familiar to me；I am familiar with the task |
| work ethics 泛化为努力工作 | a strong work ethic 表努力负责的工作态度；professional ethics 表职业伦理 |
| 第 8 篇题文错配 | 题目讨论教师薪酬；范文讨论学校设备预算且引用不存在的学生名，不能照背 |
| 未注明来源的百分比或研究 | 如手册中的“减少 50%”，不作为已核实事实背诵；优先用合理具体情境 |

## Email 的表达组织

先确定收件人、目的与题目列出的任务，再写：目的与必要背景 → 具体情况或影响 → 请求、建议或补救 → 简短结尾。

- 请求：说清希望对方做什么，必要时提出替代方案。
- 咨询：问题应具体，给对方足够背景。
- 报告问题：说明现象、时间、影响和已尝试措施。
- 改期：解释冲突并给出可选时间。
- 道歉：承担责任、补救、预防。
- 邀请：时间地点、活动内容、价值、回复期限。
- 感谢：指出具体帮助与结果。
- 推荐：说明兴趣和限制，解释推荐理由。
- 跟进：说明之前的联系、礼貌询问进展。
- 拒绝：清楚但礼貌，可行时提供替代方案。

语域：给老师或陌生服务人员可用 Dear ... 和 Best regards；给熟悉同学可用 Hi ... 和 Best。不要在轻松邀请中强套强烈投诉语气。题目未要求附件时不随意声称“已附上”。

## Discussion 的论证组织

立场 → 一个主要理由 → 解释机制 → 具体例子或对比 → 必要的条件或局限。回应同学应增加新内容，不只重复 I agree。

可用框架：
- The main reason is that ...：主要原因是……
- This matters because ...：这一点重要，是因为……
- For example, a student who ...：例如，一个……的学生。
- While I understand this concern, ...：虽然我理解这种顾虑，……
- Another factor worth considering is ...：另一个值得考虑的因素是……
- This approach is more likely to work when ...：当……时，这种方法更可能奏效。

不要把所有观点写成必然结果。可用 can、may、is likely to，并解释适用条件。具体假设例子应明确为情境，不伪称研究或统计。

## Listen and Repeat 的训练组织

先熟悉场景语块，再做只听不看文本的完整句复述。练习时可按意群理解；作答时自然连贯。特别检查否定、时间、条件、单复数与时态。不要用同义改写代替原句。

本手册的英文句子可用于浏览器语音练习。合成语音的声音、连读与真实考试录音不同，应同时练 ETS 官方音频。自行录音回听用于发现漏词与不清晰发音，不作为官方分数。

## Interview 的训练组织

第一句直接回答；再说一个理由；用经历或具体场景展开；必要时补条件。先掌握简洁的表达，再练自然衔接。

- 经历题：过去事件 → 采取的行动 → 结果或认识。
- 偏好题：选择 → 理由 → 具体例子。
- 评价题：看法 → 如何产生作用 → 限制。
- 预测题：趋势 → 驱动因素 → 条件。

## Build a Sentence 的语法速查

| 结构 | 自编例句 | 重点 |
|---|---|---|
| 间接疑问 | Could you tell me when the workshop starts? | 从句不倒装 |
| whether 从句 | I am not sure whether the room is available. | 陈述语序 |
| 定语从句 | The course that I chose includes practical projects. | 修饰名词 |
| 疑问词加不定式 | She explained how to submit the application. | how to do |
| 动名词 | I look forward to hearing from you. | to 是介词 |
| 宾补 | I found the instructions easy to follow. | find + 宾语 + 形容词 |
| 否定 | The store did not have the book I needed. | did not + 原形 |
| 主谓一致 | Reviewing examples helps me remember the structure. | 动名词主语用单数 |

### Email 写作素材

#### email-001 教育 请求帮助

- 表达：**Could you help me with**
- 中文：你能帮我处理……吗
- 例句：Could you help me with the course registration process?
- 搭配与迁移：with 后接名词或动名词，明确需要的帮助。

#### email-002 教育 申请延期

- 表达：**Would it be possible to extend**
- 中文：是否可以延长……
- 例句：Would it be possible to extend the deadline by two days?
- 搭配与迁移：by two days 表示延长两天；说明理由并给出新日期。

#### email-003 校园 请求维修

- 表达：**Could you arrange for someone to**
- 中文：能否安排人来……
- 例句：Could you arrange for someone to check the router this week?
- 搭配与迁移：arrange for someone to do；提出可执行的行动。

#### email-004 工作 请求材料

- 表达：**I would appreciate it if you could**
- 中文：如果您能……我将很感激
- 例句：I would appreciate it if you could send me the updated schedule.
- 搭配与迁移：it 不能省；后面用 could，避免重复客套。

#### email-005 研究 寻求反馈

- 表达：**Could you give me some feedback on**
- 中文：能否就……给我一些反馈
- 例句：Could you give me some feedback on the introduction to my report?
- 搭配与迁移：feedback 不可数；指定要反馈的部分。

#### email-006 活动 请求许可

- 表达：**May I have permission to**
- 中文：我可以获准……吗
- 例句：May I have permission to use the meeting room on Friday?
- 搭配与迁移：to 后接动词原形；补充使用时间与用途。

#### email-007 教育 咨询要求

- 表达：**Could you clarify whether**
- 中文：能否说明是否……
- 例句：Could you clarify whether attendance is required for the workshop?
- 搭配与迁移：whether 后用陈述语序，不用疑问倒装。

#### email-008 活动 询问名额

- 表达：**Are there any places available**
- 中文：还有名额吗
- 例句：Are there any places available in the afternoon session?
- 搭配与迁移：places 表示名额；美式英语也可说 spots。

#### email-009 校园 咨询服务

- 表达：**I would like to know more about**
- 中文：我想进一步了解……
- 例句：I would like to know more about the library borrowing policy.
- 搭配与迁移：about 后接明确项目，随后提出具体问题。

#### email-010 旅行 询问费用

- 表达：**Does the fee include**
- 中文：费用是否包括……
- 例句：Does the fee include transportation to the conference venue?
- 搭配与迁移：include 后接名词；不要写 does includes。

#### email-011 研究 确认材料

- 表达：**Could you let me know what documents I need**
- 中文：能否告知我需要哪些材料
- 例句：Could you let me know what documents I need for the application?
- 搭配与迁移：what documents I need 用陈述语序。

#### email-012 教育 询问资格

- 表达：**Am I eligible to apply for**
- 中文：我是否有资格申请……
- 例句：Am I eligible to apply for the scholarship as a first-year student?
- 搭配与迁移：eligible to do；eligible for 加项目名。

#### email-013 校园 报告故障

- 表达：**I am writing to report a problem with**
- 中文：写信报告……的问题
- 例句：I am writing to report a problem with the heating in my room.
- 搭配与迁移：with 指明设备或服务；开头直接说明目的。

#### email-014 校园 描述断线

- 表达：**The connection keeps dropping**
- 中文：网络反复断线
- 例句：The connection keeps dropping during my online classes.
- 搭配与迁移：keep doing 表示反复发生；比 vague bad 更具体。

#### email-015 消费 描述收货问题

- 表达：**The item I received does not match**
- 中文：我收到的商品与……不符
- 例句：The item I received does not match the description on your website.
- 搭配与迁移：说明具体不符之处；避免只表达不满。

#### email-016 教育 说明影响

- 表达：**This has made it difficult for me to**
- 中文：这使我难以……
- 例句：This has made it difficult for me to submit my assignments on time.
- 搭配与迁移：make it difficult for someone to do；it 不能省。

#### email-017 校园 说明持续时间

- 表达：**The problem has persisted since**
- 中文：问题从……起持续存在
- 例句：The problem has persisted since Monday morning.
- 搭配与迁移：since 加起点；for 加时长。

#### email-018 工作 说明已尝试措施

- 表达：**I have already tried**
- 中文：我已经尝试过……
- 例句：I have already tried restarting the software, but the error still appears.
- 搭配与迁移：try doing 表示尝试一种解决方法。

#### email-019 工作 改期请求

- 表达：**Could we reschedule our meeting for**
- 中文：我们能否将会议改到……
- 例句：Could we reschedule our meeting for Thursday afternoon?
- 搭配与迁移：reschedule for 加新时间；用 from A to B 表示完整变化。

#### email-020 教育 解释冲突

- 表达：**I have a scheduling conflict**
- 中文：我的时间安排有冲突
- 例句：I have a scheduling conflict with a required laboratory session.
- 搭配与迁移：说明冲突事件，不需要冗长个人信息。

#### email-021 工作 提供备选时间

- 表达：**I am available on**
- 中文：我在……有空
- 例句：I am available on Tuesday morning or Wednesday after three.
- 搭配与迁移：on 加日期；at 加时刻；给出两个选项。

#### email-022 活动 询问是否合适

- 表达：**Please let me know if either time works for you**
- 中文：请告知这两个时间是否有一个合适
- 例句：Please let me know if either time works for you.
- 搭配与迁移：either 指两者之一；works 表示安排合适。

#### email-023 教育 确认时间

- 表达：**I am writing to confirm**
- 中文：写信确认……
- 例句：I am writing to confirm our appointment for Friday at ten.
- 搭配与迁移：confirm 后直接接名词；日期时间要具体。

#### email-024 活动 提前告知

- 表达：**I wanted to let you know in advance that**
- 中文：想提前告知您……
- 例句：I wanted to let you know in advance that I will arrive a little late.
- 搭配与迁移：wanted 是礼貌语气；随后给出预计时间。

#### email-025 工作 道歉

- 表达：**I apologize for**
- 中文：我为……道歉
- 例句：I apologize for sending the wrong version of the report.
- 搭配与迁移：for 后接名词或 doing；不用 apologize to do。

#### email-026 教育 承认疏漏

- 表达：**I overlooked**
- 中文：我疏忽了……
- 例句：I overlooked the change to the submission deadline.
- 搭配与迁移：说明自己的疏漏；不推卸责任。

#### email-027 工作 补交材料

- 表达：**I have attached the corrected version**
- 中文：已附上更正后的版本
- 例句：I have attached the corrected version for your review.
- 搭配与迁移：只有实际提供附件时才使用。

#### email-028 活动 解释缺席

- 表达：**I was unable to attend because**
- 中文：我无法参加，因为……

### Interview 观点与表达

共 60 张，学习、运动、工作、科技、旅行、社交、成长、社区、时间管理、校园生活、环境、消费决策各 5 张。

每张包含访谈问题、核心表达、单句例子、原创展开回答和搭配提示。下列回答依照切题展开、准确词汇与自然口语的方向编写，未经官方评分，不对应保证分数。回答中的经历为示例，请换成自己的真实内容；实际表现还取决于流畅度、发音与现场组织。参考：[ETS Interview 评分要求](https://www.ets.org/pdfs/toefl/speaking-rubrics.pdf)。

#### interview-001 学习 偏好

- 访谈问题：Do you prefer studying alone or with other people?
- 表达：**work at my own pace**
- 中文：按自己的节奏学习
- 例句：I prefer studying alone because I can work at my own pace.
- 搭配与迁移：搭配：at my own pace；holding anyone up 表示耽误别人。展开：先给偏好，再用一道具体难题说明，最后交代小组学习的适用场景。

原创高分导向示例：

I generally prefer studying alone because I can work at my own pace. If I'm struggling with a statistics problem, I can spend an extra ten minutes on it without holding anyone up. I still meet classmates before exams to compare approaches, but I get the most focused work done on my own.

#### interview-002 学习 习惯

原创高分导向示例：

I try to break a large task into smaller steps before I start. For a research paper, that means choosing a question first, then finding sources, and only then writing a draft. Finishing one small step gives me a clear sense of progress. It also makes it easier to spot problems while I still have time to fix them.

原创高分导向示例：

I usually review the feedback so I can learn from my mistakes. In one essay, my examples were relevant, but I hadn't explained how they supported my argument. For the next assignment, I added a sentence after each example to make that connection clear. That was more useful than simply rereading the textbook and hoping for a better result.

Traditional news sources also need to be evaluated, so the most useful habit is checking evidence and comparing reliable sources rather than assuming that one type of platform is always trustworthy.

### task-015 环保责任如何分担

类型：Discussion 写作素材｜领域：环境｜建议练习时间：600 秒

教授问：环保应主要依靠企业改变生产方式，还是个人改变生活习惯？同学分别支持两方。明确责任分工并举例。

自查要点：
- 明确责任分工
- 解释影响机制
- 具体例子
- 避免假设个人或企业能单独解决所有问题

参考回答：

I think businesses should take greater responsibility for changes that consumers cannot make on their own. Individuals can reduce waste, but they usually have little control over how a product is packaged or repaired. A company can influence these decisions across thousands of purchases. For example, making replacement parts available allows customers to repair a device instead of buying a new one. Individual participation still matters because people need to choose and use the repair option. However, that choice must exist first. The most effective approach therefore combines business changes that make sustainable behavior practical with information that helps consumers use those options, rather than placing the entire burden on either group.

### task-016 是否购买最新科技产品

类型：Discussion 写作素材｜领域：消费｜建议练习时间：600 秒

教授问：成为新科技产品的早期使用者有什么优势或劣势？同学甲关注价格，同学乙关注反馈机会。发展一个新理由。

自查要点：
- 至少一个明确优点或缺点
- 说明真实使用条件
- 具体例子
- 不夸大所有产品的风险

参考回答：

One disadvantage of buying a new device immediately is that it may be difficult to judge how well it fits your actual needs. Advertisements usually highlight new features, but independent reviews and long-term user feedback may still be limited. For example, a student buying a tablet for taking notes needs to know whether its software works reliably with the files used in class. A feature that looks impressive in a demonstration may not solve that practical problem. Early adopters can provide useful feedback, as another student notes, but consumers with a limited budget may benefit more from waiting until they can compare experiences and make an informed decision.

### task-017 结伴运动

类型：Interview 观点与表达｜领域：运动｜建议练习时间：45 秒

Would you prefer to exercise alone or with other people? Explain your choice with an example.

自查要点：
- 直接选择
- 一个理由
- 具体个人例子
- 说清例子如何支撑理由

参考回答：

I prefer exercising with a friend because we keep each other motivated. Last semester, a classmate and I planned two short runs each week. When I felt like skipping a workout, knowing that someone was waiting for me made it easier to go. We did not have to run at the same speed the whole time; we simply agreed on when to start. That routine helped me exercise more consistently during a busy semester. I still enjoy walking alone sometimes, but for regular exercise, having a partner works better for me.

### task-018 学习习惯

类型：Interview 观点与表达｜领域：学习｜建议练习时间：45 秒

What is one study habit that works well for you? Explain why it helps.

自查要点：
- 一个习惯
- 具体实施步骤
- 效果
- 避免罗列太多不展开的习惯

参考回答：

One habit that works well for me is breaking a large task into smaller steps. If I need to write a report, I first decide on the main question, then collect examples, and finally draft one section at a time. This makes the work feel more manageable. For example, I used this approach for a group presentation last month, and it helped me notice early that we needed more evidence. I also leave time to review the final version. The habit helps because I always know what to do next instead of worrying about the entire task.

### task-019 灵活工作安排

类型：Interview 观点与表达｜领域：工作｜建议练习时间：45 秒

Would flexible working hours affect your interest in a job? Why or why not?

自查要点：
- 直接表态
- 说明影响因素
- 假设或真实例子
- 承认岗位需要

参考回答：

Yes, flexible hours would make a job more attractive to me because I would have more control over my schedule. For instance, starting earlier could help me avoid a crowded commute and leave time for exercise in the evening. I think that would make my daily routine easier to maintain. Of course, flexibility should not mean being unavailable when colleagues need help. I would still expect clear deadlines and agreed times for meetings. As long as the work allows it, this balance would matter to me when choosing between otherwise similar jobs.

### task-020 学习应用评价

类型：Interview 观点与表达｜领域：科技｜建议练习时间：45 秒

Do you think apps are a good way to organize learning goals? Explain your view.

自查要点：
- 给出评价
- 解释工具用途
- 具体例子
- 说明使用边界

参考回答：

I think a simple app can be useful because it helps me keep track of my progress. For example, I can record which expressions I have practiced and which ones I need to review. That makes the next study session easier to plan. However, I would avoid an app that sends constant notifications or takes too much time to manage. The purpose is to support learning, not to turn planning into another task. For me, an app works best when I can check it quickly and then spend most of my time actually practicing.

## 每天 20–30 分钟的卡片计划

### 新卡与复习节奏

- 周一至周六：每天目标新学 16 张，搭配到期旧卡复习，默认 30 分钟。
- 周日：不安排新卡，只复习薄弱卡和做卡片主动回忆。
- 初次学习：理解含义和搭配，听或读例句，然后遮住英文主动回忆。

- 实际负担优先于日历：漏学的卡片顺延，第二天不翻倍；不把到期数量当作必须当天全部清空的债务。
